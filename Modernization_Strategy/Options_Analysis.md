# Modernization Options Analysis

| Strategy | Cost | Risk | Timeline | Debt Reduction | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retain | Low | High | Short | Low | Reject |
| Rehost | Medium | Medium | Short | Low | Partial |
| Replatform | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium/High | Select |
| Refactor | High | High | Long | High | Selectively use |
| Replace | High | High | Long | High | Reject for scope |
| Retire | Low | Low | Medium | High | Use for obsolete components |

Selected approach: replatform infrastructure/data, selectively refactor integration and reporting components, retire obsolete servers/jobs.
