# Incident Response Objectives

1. Establish scope and severity within 60 minutes of declaration.
2. Contain prioritized identities and indicators within four hours.
3. Preserve relevant authentication, IAM, endpoint, and network evidence.
4. Determine whether suspicious successful authentication represents compromise.
5. Investigate outbound-transfer anomalies without overstating conclusions.
6. Restore legitimate access only after credential and MFA validation.
7. Maintain scheduled executive and technical communications.
8. Track risks, issues, decisions, actions, and changes to closure.
9. Complete root-cause and control-gap analysis.
10. Transfer preventive actions into owned operational work.
