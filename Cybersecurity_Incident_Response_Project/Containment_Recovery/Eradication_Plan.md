# Eradication Plan

1. Remove or invalidate compromised credentials and sessions.
2. Review account permissions and remove unauthorized grants.
3. Confirm no new privileged identities, tokens, or persistence mechanisms exist in scoped telemetry.
4. Apply MFA and authentication-policy improvements.
5. Remove temporary emergency access after validation.
6. Confirm blocked indicators remain effective.
7. Re-run targeted searches for suspicious authentication and access.

Exit criterion: no known active unauthorized access path remains within scenario scope.
