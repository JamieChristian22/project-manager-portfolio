# Containment Plan

## Immediate (0-4 hours)
Lock Tier-1 accounts; invalidate sessions; reset credentials; block high-confidence suspicious sources; preserve logs; raise monitoring; review privileged identities.

## Short-Term (4-24 hours)
Expand identity review; enforce MFA; validate endpoints associated with suspicious success; correlate outbound activity; tune alerts; restrict unnecessary access.

## Guardrails
Every disruptive action has an owner and rollback path. Avoid broad network blocking without validation. Preserve evidence before destructive remediation. Communicate user impact.

## Completion
All Tier-1 identities secured, critical indicators blocked, no uncontrolled active suspicious sessions, and investigation can proceed without immediate expansion of exposure.
