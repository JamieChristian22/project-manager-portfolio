# Post-Incident Review

## Summary
IR-2026-008 was declared after abnormal authentication activity showed multi-account automated attack characteristics. The team contained prioritized identities and sources, investigated a suspicious successful login and outbound anomaly, restored access through controlled recovery, and implemented authentication and SIEM improvements.

## Response Evaluation
Detection: effective but correlation can be earlier. Containment: completed inside scenario objective. Communications: confirmed facts and uncertainties separated. Recovery: gated and validated. Governance: all critical actions and risks had owners.

## Final Determination
Credential-attack activity was credible and required response. The scenario evidence did not justify claiming confirmed data exfiltration.
