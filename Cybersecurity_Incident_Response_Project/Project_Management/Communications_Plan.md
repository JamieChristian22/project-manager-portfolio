# Communications Plan

## Technical Bridge
Cadence: every 60 minutes during active containment. Content: new evidence, actions, blockers, owners, next decision.

## Executive Update
Cadence: 13:00 and 17:00 Day 1, then daily until stable. Format: what happened, confirmed impact, unconfirmed risks, actions completed, decisions needed, recovery outlook.

## User Communication
Only affected users receive credential/MFA instructions. Do not disclose unnecessary technical details or speculate about breach status.

## Legal/Compliance Trigger
Engage immediately if confirmed sensitive-data access/exfiltration, regulated records, or notification thresholds are implicated.
