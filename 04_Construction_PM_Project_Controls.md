**Purpose:** Demonstrate job-ready construction project management,

> **Portfolio disclosure:** This is a hands-on professional simulation
> created for portfolio and interview demonstration. It is not
> represented as paid construction employment.

## Executive Portfolio Summary

This project simulates the management of a complex corporate commercial
construction program from preconstruction through turnover. The control
system integrates schedule, budget, procurement, design coordination,
change management, field execution, safety, quality, stakeholder
communication, commissioning, and closeout.

The project manager's role is to maintain a reliable management system
that converts field and commercial information into decisions. The
portfolio therefore focuses on the controls a corporate PM would use to
identify variance, assign ownership, escalate risks, protect milestones,
manage contingency, document decisions, and forecast final outcomes.

## Interview Use

Use this project to discuss how you would manage a real corporate
construction assignment. Strong interview stories include:

-   Protecting the weather-tight milestone through schedule recovery and
    resequencing.
-   Monitoring critical-path work and predecessor relationships rather
    than relying only on percent complete.
-   Managing long-lead electrical equipment through procurement
    milestones and factory follow-up.
-   Controlling RFIs and submittals so unresolved design decisions do
    not delay field installation.
-   Evaluating PCOs and approved changes against contingency and
    forecast-at-completion.
-   Using risk, issue, safety, and QA/QC registers to assign
    accountability and prevent downstream impact.
-   Reporting schedule, cost, procurement, safety, quality, and
    executive decisions through one integrated control system.
-   Planning turnover before the end of construction through
    commissioning, punch, O&M manuals, training, warranties, as-builts,
    and financial closeout.
