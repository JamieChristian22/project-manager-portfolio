# Hypercare & Operational Handoff
## Hypercare
Three business days after cutover. Twice-daily review of incidents, availability, latency, error rates, capacity, backup jobs, and user reports. Exit when no Sev-1/2 incident remains, KPIs are stable, and Operations accepts ownership.
## Handoff Package
Architecture; configuration baseline; monitoring thresholds; alert routing; backup/restore instructions; incident escalation; known issues; access ownership; cost-review cadence; vendor/AWS support path; change-management process.
## Training
60-minute operations walkthrough plus 30-minute Q&A. Operations demonstrates ability to locate dashboard, acknowledge an alarm, find the runbook, and identify rollback/restore contacts.
