FR-002 Validate provider availability.

FR-004 Apply visit duration.  
FR-005 Prevent unauthorized double-booking.  
FR-006 Manage cancellations/reschedules.  
FR-007 Manage waitlist.  
FR-008 Support urgent slots.  
FR-009 Send confirmations/reminders.  
FR-010 Track no-shows.
