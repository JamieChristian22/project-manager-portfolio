# Business Case

## Problem
Fragmented scheduling caused long booking times, double-booking risk, low visibility, and inconsistent resource use.

## Recommendation
Implement a centralized scheduling system for 120 users across Primary Care, Cardiology, Imaging, and Orthopedics.

## Expected Benefits
Faster scheduling, fewer conflicts, better provider/room utilization, lower no-show rate, stronger reporting, and improved patient access.

## Scenario Financial Case
Budget: $265,000. Modeled annual operational benefit: $310,000. Approximate first-year gross ROI: 17.0%.
