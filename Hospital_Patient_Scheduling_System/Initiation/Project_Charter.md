## Purpose
Implement a centralized hospital scheduling platform.

## Success Criteria
- Schedule variance ≤5%
- Budget variance ≤5%
- UAT pass ≥95%
- 0 Sev 1 defects at go-live
- Training ≥95%
- 30-day adoption ≥90%
- Average scheduling time ≤7 minutes
- Double-booking <1%
