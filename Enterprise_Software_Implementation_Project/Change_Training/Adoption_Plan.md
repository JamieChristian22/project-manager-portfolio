# Adoption Plan

## Adoption Measures
Weekly active users, opportunities updated, overdue opportunities, required-field completeness, dashboard usage, training completion.

## Targets
30-day active adoption ≥90%; required-field completeness ≥95%.

## Interventions
Manager coaching, targeted refresher training, office hours, quick tips, support follow-up.

## Scenario Result
30-day active adoption: 92%.
