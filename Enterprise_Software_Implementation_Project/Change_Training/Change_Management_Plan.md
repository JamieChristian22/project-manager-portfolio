# Change Management Plan

## Change Impacts
Users move from spreadsheets/email tracking to Salesforce as the system of record.

## Strategy
- Sponsor messaging
- Manager alignment
- Change champions
- Role-based training
- FAQs and quick guides
- Office hours
- Hypercare
- Adoption dashboard

## Resistance Themes
Time required for data entry, new process expectations, and concern about visibility.

## Response
Explain business value, simplify page layouts, train by role, provide manager reinforcement, and monitor usage.
