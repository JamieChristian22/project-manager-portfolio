# Discovery Summary

Discovery interviews included Sales Operations, sales managers, representatives, IT support, and leadership.

## Key Findings
- Stage definitions varied by manager.
- Opportunity ownership was inconsistent.
- Weekly pipeline reporting required manual consolidation.
- Duplicate customer records were common.
- Required fields were not consistently enforced.
- Management lacked a shared dashboard.
- Follow-up activity was difficult to audit.

## Priorities
P1: opportunity process, data ownership, migration, access, dashboards.  
P2: automation and advanced report refinements.  
P3: future integrations and enhancement backlog.
