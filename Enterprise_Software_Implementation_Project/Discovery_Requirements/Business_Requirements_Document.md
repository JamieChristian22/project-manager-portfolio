# Business Requirements Document

## Business Requirements
BR-001 Centralize accounts, contacts, leads, and opportunities.  
BR-002 Standardize opportunity stages.  
BR-003 Provide manager and executive pipeline dashboards.  
BR-004 Reduce duplicate and incomplete records.  
BR-005 Support role-based access.  
BR-006 Capture sales activities.  
BR-007 Support forecast-ready opportunity data.  
BR-008 Provide governed reports without manual spreadsheet consolidation.  
BR-009 Migrate approved legacy data.  
BR-010 Provide training and support for launch.

## Acceptance
Requirements approved as the simulated release baseline; later additions require change control.
