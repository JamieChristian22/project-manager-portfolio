# Business Case

## Problem
Sales teams use spreadsheets and email to manage customer and opportunity data, creating inconsistent stages, duplicate records, slow reporting, and weak forecasting.

## Recommended Investment
Implement Salesforce CRM for 75 users with standardized sales processes, centralized records, dashboards, role-based access, and governed data migration.

## Strategic Benefits
- Single source of truth
- Standardized opportunity lifecycle
- Improved management reporting
- Reduced manual reporting
- Better data ownership
- Scalable foundation for future automation

## Scenario Financial Case
Implementation budget: $185,000. Modeled annual benefit: $200,000. First-year gross ROI: 8.1%, with stronger value expected in later years because major implementation costs are one-time.

## Approval Recommendation
Proceed with a 16-week implementation using Hybrid Agile delivery and gated approval for requirements, UAT, and go-live.
