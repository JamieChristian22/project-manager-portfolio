# Business Case

## Problem
The legacy business system suffers from poor performance, technical debt, fragile integrations, limited monitoring, manual reporting, and increasing maintenance cost.

## Recommendation
Modernize the system through a hybrid replatform and selective-refactor strategy while retiring obsolete components.

## Strategic Benefits
- Improved reliability and performance
- Reduced infrastructure and support cost
- Better monitoring and recoverability
- Stronger security
- Faster reporting
- Reduced manual processing
- Lower end-of-life risk

## Scenario Financial Case
Budget: $425,000. Modeled annual benefit: $505,000. Approximate first-year gross ROI: 18.8%.

## Approval
Proceed with a 24-week staged modernization program.
